How to install on Android
1. Open this page in Chrome on your Android phone.
2. Tap the three-dot menu (top right).
3. Tap "Add to Home screen" → Install.
4. Open from your home screen — it runs like a real app.
5. Tap Activate Today's Alarms and allow notifications when prompted.
6. Keep Chrome running in background — do not force-stop it.

The alarm picks two random SDG numbers and notifies you at two random times within the day. New random times are picked each time you activate.
